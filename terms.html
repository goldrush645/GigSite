Terms & conditions

Design prototype disclosure (9 November 2026). This page explains the current demonstration, not a final set of legally reviewed terms for a live commercial service.

Demonstration only

Events are sourced from real official event listings, but coverage is incomplete and information may become outdated. Always confirm dates, venues, ticket availability and event conditions directly with the linked provider before purchasing tickets or travelling. ENCORE does not currently verify announcement dates or resale availability.

No ticket purchases or alerts

Following artists and festivals, and saving reminders, store browser-local preferences only. ENCORE currently does not monitor ticket inventory, send emails or facilitate ticket sales.

Third-party ticket marketplaces

Official event links lead to independent ticket providers with their own terms. ENCORE is not affiliated with those providers and does not guarantee ticket availability. Resale listings are not yet integrated.

Before public launch

A production service needs operator contact details, reviewed consumer terms, a privacy policy, provider permissions and clear procedures for disputes and data requests.

Privacy

Design prototype disclosure (9 November 2026). This page describes the current ENCORE demonstration only. It is not a final privacy policy for a public production service.

What this prototype stores

The website saves followed artists, city choice, festival follows and locally saved reminders in your own browser's localStorage. It does not have user accounts, a server-side database or working notification deliveries.

External services

The website loads an event feed with listings from official Moshtix pages, plus a scheduled server-side Moshtix event retrieval when enabled. Clicking an official ticket link opens the independent ticket provider, which has its own privacy policy. Resale inventory is not integrated and no tickets or payments are processed by ENCORE.

How to remove demo preferences

Clear the site's browser data / local storage to remove your saved demonstration choices. Data may also disappear if your browser clears site storage.

Before public launch

A production version needs a reviewed policy naming the actual service operator and contact details, hosting and analytics providers, personal information collected, notification providers and retention/deletion procedures.
